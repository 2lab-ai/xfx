# The surviving mutants, and why each is equivalent.

## reset-per-word (file_rows_painted put back to six for each new @ word)
Survives rounds 2 and 3, and it is equivalent:
- A new word's FilePicker starts untrusted, so its keys are spent until its
  first frame lands (file_completion_state.zig:175, :239-240@481104fd), and
  that frame sets the count before any key reads it. The count's one reader
  (offer_to_files' step) is behind that gate (opus, round 2).
- The count is fx's `visible_rows`, kept in the picker state for the
  session (only deinit resets it, :121-128). No key in fx reads it across
  words either.

## landed-any-slot -- not equivalent (round 2 said it was; opus round 2, MUST 2)
The read-only panel outranks a list that a paste opens under it: the panel
comes first in Shell::slot, and reconcile_picker opens the list behind it.
Round 2's probe tried Ctrl-P, `/provider @s` and `/mcp show @s`, not the
panel, and wrongly concluded that no surface outranks a live list.
Round 3's test kills the mutant:
shell::a_frame_that_shows_another_surface_over_the_list_neither_trusts_it_nor_sets_its_rows
(mutants-run4.txt).
