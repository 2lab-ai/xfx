1. Where fx settles a paste: at the first empty readiness check
   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:92-107     pollInput(timeout), then up to 32 reads of 128 bytes while
                                      pollInput(0) is readable
   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:109-112    still readable after 32 reads: commit a frame, `continue`,
                                      unsettled
   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:113-116    closed: the session ends
   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:118        otherwise settle_delivery_epoch -- the next poll that finds
                                      nothing, however soon the terminal writes again
   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:95-99      read_len == 0: `.input_closed`, no settle
   => fx has no notion of quiet: the first empty poll after the marker settles, and the
      bytes after it are decoded as keys. That is the boundary PASTE-GAP moves.
