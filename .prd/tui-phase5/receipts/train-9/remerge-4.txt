Merge HISTORY-NOTICE-STOP (fix/p5-history-notice-stop @ 62d96c98) into train 9

A history notice is said once it is handed to the screen, not when it
is found: Conversation::unsaid only finds the unsaid lines,
Conversation::said records one shown; the TUI records a line once
send_ui hands it on, the line shell once its stderr line is written.
A turn stopped before its request leaves the notice to the next turn.

Conflict: src/tui/worker.rs, the end of the tests module: train 9's
join test (a_stop_in_the_catalog_wait_of_a_turn_that_would_ask_twenty_questions_ends_it_as_the_stop
and its helpers) and HISTORY-NOTICE-STOP's tests were both appended
there, and git interleaved them in two hunks around lines they share.
Both kept whole, the join test first: the file is the base plus each
side's change (272/14 and 240/0 lines against 05a0a857, as each side
has them).
