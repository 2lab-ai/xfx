   - src/ui/event_loop.zig:94-119               the delivery epoch: up to 32 reads of 128 bytes while

   => "after" = every byte from the one after the end marker's `~` until the terminal has nothing

6. Edge cases
   - a second paste in the same delivery: its `ESC[200~` arrives while end_candidate, so it is counted
     (paste_framing.zig:102-107) and never begins a paste (beginPaste is reached only from the key decoder,
     app_input_runtime.zig:932-944, which an active paste bypasses, main.zig:3135-3138); its content and its
     own end marker are counted too (the test at :527-546 counts a second end marker).
   - an Escape sequence behind the end: the same, counted, not decoded; fx's e2e
     tests/e2e/tui-composer-edit-contracts.test.ts in 9e0eab41 counts `ESC[` behind a paste as
     "bytes=8" with ESCAPE_DRAFTpaste kept.
   - the end marker split across reads or deliveries: advanceEndMatcher keeps its count
     (paste_framing.zig:299-312) and settlement is `none` while capturing (:161-162, test :502-511),
     so a split marker is the marker and the paste settles in the delivery that completes it.
