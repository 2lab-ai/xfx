xfx shell commands
  /help     show available slash commands
  /clear    clear the screen; the conversation and its session are kept
  /new      start a fresh session
  /setup    `/setup <gateway|llmux>` to switch provider and record the choice
  /model    show the model, or `/model <id>` to use another one from now on
  /version  show the xfx version
  /quit     exit the interactive shell (also /exit)
Anything else is a prompt. Ctrl-C stops a running turn; Ctrl-D leaves.
